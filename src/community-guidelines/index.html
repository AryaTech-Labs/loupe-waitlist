Community Guidelines

Loupe — Arya Tech Inc. • Effective: March 22, 2026

“Any watch welcome. Every watch celebrated.”

Loupe is a community built on a shared passion for watches. These guidelines exist to keep our platform welcoming, trustworthy, and enjoyable for every member—from first-time Casio enthusiasts to seasoned Patek Philippe collectors.

1. Respect All Collectors

Every watch and every collector has a story. We celebrate the full spectrum of horology.

  • Treat all members with respect, regardless of the watches they own or their budget
  • A $50 Seiko 5 deserves the same enthusiasm as a $50,000 Rolex Daytona
  • Constructive discussion is welcome; elitism, gatekeeping, and dismissive comments are not
  • Respect differing opinions about brands, styles, and collecting philosophies

2. Be Authentic

Honesty builds trust. Our community thrives on genuine interactions.

  • Only post watches you actually own, have handled, or are reviewing with disclosure
  • Be truthful about your collection, ownership history, and watch knowledge
  • Do not impersonate other collectors, dealers, brand representatives, or Loupe staff
  • If you are affiliated with a brand or dealer, disclose it in your bio and relevant posts

3. Marketplace Integrity

Trust is the foundation of our marketplace. We enforce strict standards to protect every transaction.

  • No counterfeits — listing a counterfeit, replica, or “homage” watch as genuine is strictly prohibited and results in immediate suspension
  • Accurate descriptions — describe condition, provenance, and included accessories honestly. Use our standardized condition grades (Mint, Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair)
  • Honor your commitments — if you accept an offer or complete a sale, follow through. Repeatedly failing to fulfill transactions may result in account restrictions
  • Fair pricing — while you set your own prices, predatory pricing practices (such as price gouging during shortages) are discouraged
  • Photos must be of the actual watch — stock photos or photos of a different unit are not permitted in listings

4. Content Standards

Keep Loupe a place everyone can enjoy.

  • No hate speech — content that promotes hatred, discrimination, or violence against any individual or group is prohibited
  • No explicit content — sexually explicit, gratuitously violent, or graphic content is not permitted
  • No spam — do not post repetitive content, unsolicited promotions, or irrelevant links. Self-promotion is fine when it adds value to the community
  • Stay on topic — Loupe is about watches and the culture around them. Off-topic content may be removed
  • No harassment — targeted abuse, threats, doxxing, or sustained unwanted contact is grounds for immediate ban

5. Club Guidelines

Watch Clubs are community spaces organized around shared interests.

  • Follow these Community Guidelines in all club interactions
  • Respect club moderators and their decisions
  • Keep discussions relevant to the club’s topic and brand focus
  • Do not use clubs primarily for commercial promotion without contributing to the community

6. Reporting & Enforcement

If you see something that violates these guidelines, report it. Every post, listing, profile, and comment has a report option. We review all reports and take action based on severity:

  • Content removal — violating content is removed and the author is notified
  • Warning — for first-time or minor violations, we issue a formal warning
  • Temporary suspension — repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary account suspension
  • Permanent ban — severe violations (counterfeits, fraud, harassment, illegal activity) result in permanent removal from the platform

We apply enforcement consistently regardless of a member’s follower count, transaction volume, or the value of their collection.

7. Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action was made in error, you may appeal by contacting us at:
support@neuroforgetech.com

Include your username, the action taken, and why you believe it should be reconsidered. We review all appeals and will respond within 5 business days.

8. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these Community Guidelines as our community grows. We will notify members of material changes through an in-app notification. Continued use of Loupe after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated guidelines.
