Privacy Policy

Loupe — Arya Tech Inc. • Effective: March 22, 2026

Arya Tech Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates Loupe, a social commerce platform for watch enthusiasts. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our iOS application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address, username, and display name
  • Profile photo and cover photo
  • Bio, website URL, and social links

User Content

  • Posts, photos, videos, and stories you create
  • Comments and reviews
  • Marketplace listings, offers, and transaction details
  • Watch club activity and membership
  • Watch collection and grail list entries
  • Direct messages between users

Watch Data

  • Brand preferences selected during onboarding
  • Watch DNA information (brand, model, reference number, year, movement type, case material)

Device & Usage Information

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Crash reports collected via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Usage analytics collected via Amplitude (no personally identifiable information is included in analytics events)

Transaction Data

  • Purchase and sale records processed through Stripe
  • Transaction amounts, fees, and status
  • Shipping information (city and country level only)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate the app — authenticate your account, display your profile, and provide core features including the social feed, collections, marketplace, and clubs
  • Personalize your experience — recommend content and users based on your watch preferences and engagement history
  • Facilitate the marketplace — process transactions, hold funds in escrow, connect buyers with sellers, and match grail list items with new listings
  • Send notifications — alert you about grail matches, offers, new followers, comments, and transaction updates
  • Improve the service — analyze usage patterns to improve features, fix bugs, and optimize performance
  • Ensure safety — detect and prevent fraud, counterfeit listings, and policy violations

3. Third-Party Sharing

We share your information only with the following service providers, and only as necessary to operate Loupe:

  • Supabase — backend infrastructure for data storage and authentication, with row-level security enforced at the database level
  • Stripe — payment processing for marketplace transactions (Stripe receives only the data necessary to process payments)
  • Apple — App Store distribution, push notifications via APNs, and subscription management via StoreKit
  • Firebase — crash reporting via Crashlytics (no personally identifiable information included)

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Measures include:

  • All data is stored in Supabase with row-level security (RLS) policies enforcing access controls at the database level
  • Sensitive credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain — never in UserDefaults or plain text
  • All API communications use HTTPS encryption
  • EXIF metadata (including GPS coordinates) is automatically stripped from all uploaded photos
  • Private data fields (purchase prices, personal notes, target prices) are protected by RLS and never exposed to other users

5. Children’s Privacy

Loupe is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete it promptly. The app is rated 17+ on the App Store.

6. Your Rights

  • Access — view your data through your profile, collection, and transaction history
  • Edit — update your profile, collection entries, and listings at any time
  • Delete — remove individual posts, listings, or collection entries
  • Delete your account — available in Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently removed within 30 days
  • Data portability — contact us to request a copy of your data

7. Data Retention

  • Stories automatically expire and are deleted after 24 hours
  • When you delete your account, all associated data is removed within 30 days
  • Transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required for financial and legal compliance

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through an in-app notification or by updating the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
